Jay Moon, PhD

Applied Scientist | Biosignals & Algorithms

I am an applied scientist and biomedical engineer focused on turning noisy biosignal and time-series data into reliable clinical algorithms and software tools. My work spans EEG, ICU monitoring, and medical imaging where real-world signal drift and data scarcity break standard models.

At the University of Manitoba (Section of Neurosurgery), I build standardized ICU data pipelines, lead software tooling (SPARC), and develop anomaly detection methods (DASL). Across commercial SaMD, early-stage medical AI, and technical advisory, I focus on practical signal reliability and honest validation.

Selected Work

Clinical Time-Series & Anomaly Detection (University of Manitoba)

  • Built standardized ingestion, sync, and QC pipelines for 100 Hz multi-channel ICU physiological data across 158 PHIA-compliant neurosurgical datasets (~1,400 patient-hours), and mentored 8 trainees on biosignal workflows.
  • Developed a nonlinear state anomaly detection method (DASL; Patent Pending #64/158,894) for noisy ICU signals, achieving ~4 hours of early warning and dose-response tracking over standard threshold alarms.
  • Architected and shipped SPARC (CIPO Copyright #1248739), a standalone visualization and annotation tool used by 12 clinicians (>80k labeled events), leading a small dev team to render 90M+ rows under 4 GB active RAM.
  • Led technical discovery across 36 clinical and medtech stakeholders (with Mitacs Lab-to-Market support), turning prototypes into patent disclosures and 13 pilot or collaboration inquiries.

Data-Efficient Medical ML (Datumology / North Forge)

  • Built a statistical generative augmentation method for brain CT anomaly segmentation in MONAI, matching baseline performance using 50% less training data and 50% smaller models.
  • Completed early-stage IP and regulatory scoping through North Forge, established a hospital collaboration MOU, and secured follow-on R&D funding at the University of Manitoba.

EEG SaMD & Closed-Loop Systems (NeuroCatch & U of T)

  • At NeuroCatch, investigated field performance gaps in an FDA- and Health Canada-cleared EEG SaMD and built backend signal-processing updates that raised detection accuracy above 92% across concussion, sleep, and healthy cohorts.
  • Codified clinical ERP criteria (P300, N100, N400) into deterministic scoring rules for reproducible validation, and audited validation pipelines with software and QA teams to catch ground-truth and circularity issues.
  • During my PhD at the University of Toronto and Holland Bloorview, built a real-time closed-loop EEG BCI (~75% mean online accuracy, 93% peak; N=10) and a passive speech-perception training method to reduce calibration burden and labeling effort.

Technical Advisory

  • Advised strategy and product teams (McKinsey & Co., De:Mind, Card79) as a subject matter expert on biosignal sensor limits, signal reliability, and commercial neurotechnology feasibility.
